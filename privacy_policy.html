Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the use of our mobile application P2MC (the "App"), a utility tool designed to look up local cash exchange rates for tourists and travelers alike. The App is committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App collects absolutely no personal data.

  • Personal Information: We do not require, collect, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.
  • Account Registration: The App does not feature any user account creation, profile generation, or registration systems. Therefore, no credentials or personal profiles are maintained.
  • Usage Queries: Any searches or lookups performed within the App for currency exchange rates are processed locally or transiently on a strict transactional basis. These queries are anonymous and are never tied back to an individual user's identity.

2. Device Permissions

The App requires an active internet connection to retrieve real-time exchange rates once a day for a few minutes. With your permission the app will access the camera so it can scan the price and display your target currency. With your permission it will use the microphone to listen and for the price of an item and display your target currency. You can revoke these permissions at any time. Other than the camera and microphone, This app does not access your device's precise GPS location, contacts, local storage, or other sensitive device permissions.

3. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

We value a clean, tracker-free experience. The App does not use any third-party software development kits (SDKs) for tracking, advertising, or behavior analytics.

  • No Google AdMob or advertising networks are integrated.
  • No Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or equivalent behavior-tracking tools are used.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no data from any user, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, in strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policies.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by a change in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any updates.
